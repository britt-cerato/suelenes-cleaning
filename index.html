What to Expect

A few things Suelene and her team like to go over before that first visit.

Supplies Included

The team brings all their own cleaning supplies and equipment, plus washcloths and towels that they leave right at your home. If you'd rather they use something specific, just mention it beforehand.

Meet-and-Greet First

Before the first cleaning, Suelene likes to stop by, see the space, and talk through what matters most to you.

A Real Team, Not a Solo Act

Suelene works with a team of trusted cleaners, so your appointment never depends on one person's schedule. If someone's out sick, your clean still happens.

About Suelene

Suelene used to clean houses for someone else, and it always bothered her how little care went into the work. She knew she could do better, so she did. That's really how this whole thing got started.

These days she and her team clean homes all over New Hampshire and treat every one like it's their own. Whether you need a one-time deep clean or want the team stopping by every couple of weeks, you'll get the same care every time.
